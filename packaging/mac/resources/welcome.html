ClaudeTune 2 preview

Real-time vocal pitch correction, plus the new Edit tab (capture and correct a vocal, played back in place).

This installs:

  • VST3 → /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
  • Audio Unit → /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components (Logic Pro, GarageBand)
  • Standalone app → /Applications

After installing, restart your DAW (or rescan plug-ins) so it finds ClaudeTune.
